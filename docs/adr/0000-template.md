# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Spec:** §N (where the spec covers this)

## Context

What forces this decision? Constraints, requirements, and the problem being solved.
State facts, not conclusions. If a priority ordering resolved the question, name it.

## Decision

What we are doing, stated plainly and in the active voice.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what this commits us to. Include the
negative consequences — a record listing only benefits is a sales pitch, not a decision.

## Alternatives considered

**Alternative.** Why it was rejected. Be specific enough that someone proposing it again
can see whether their new information actually changes the analysis.
